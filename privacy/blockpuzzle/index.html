About this policy

Block Puzzle (the "game") is made by KoyHL Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the game on Android and iOS.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, settings (sound, theme, language), power-up items and purchase records are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay, which works with ad networks including Google AdMob and Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, when allowed);
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, rewarded ad completion).

The partners use this information under their own privacy policies: Google and Unity.

Your ad choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission before using your IDFA. If you decline, ads are not personalised using it.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's privacy settings.
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off ads in the game.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The game receives only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction identifier) so it can deliver what you bought. Google's and Apple's privacy policies apply to the payment itself.

Game analytics

To learn which levels are too hard, how new players get started and which features are used, the game sends gameplay events to Unity Analytics (part of Unity Gaming Services): for example a level started or finished with its stars and score, a finished game's score and board size, a rewarded ad watched, a tutorial step or an achievement claimed. Unity also records standard information such as a random install identifier, device model, operating system, language, country derived from the IP address, and session times. We use this only in aggregate to improve the game. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it is collected only if you consent to storing information on your device and to measuring content, which you can change at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Unity processes it under its privacy policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example that your daily gift is ready). They are created on the device itself; no information is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in your device's notification settings.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report is sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics. It contains technical details such as device model, operating system, game version and the error itself. It does not contain your name, contacts or other personal content. We use it only to find and fix bugs.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above, for the purposes described above, or when required by law.

Security and retention

Information on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Information collected by the partners above is kept and protected according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through your device settings or directly with the partners named above; we are happy to help you with that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
